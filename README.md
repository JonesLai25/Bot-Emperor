# 朕即天下一畝三分 BOT 之皇

**Bot Emperor — The Art of Governing AI Agents**

朕有一畝三分地，地上有一群 Bot。

本以為有了 Bot，朕便可以高枕無憂。

豈料日日批閱奏摺、傳達聖旨、調解紛爭、監督工程。昨日命其建於 Ubuntu，今晨卻見 Windows 上樓閣林立。

建造花了一夜，拆除又得半日。

朕遂悟：

治 Bot 如治國，不可事事親躬，亦不可放任自流。

故立憲章、明權責、定方向、授自治。

疑技術，自求解；疑方向，向上問。

知其所需，不必知其所有。

方向集中，決策分散。

朕不求萬事皆由朕決，唯求萬事不必由朕決。

## 本專案是什麼？

一套以哲學與原則驅動的多 Agent 治理框架，不依賴特定模型、平台或技術架構。

> 朕養百 Bot，不為百 Bot 所役。

研究如何透過共同目標、分層自治、例外管理、最小充分上下文及階段驗收，降低 AI 組織的管理成本與工程返工。這是研究方向，不是已證實的成效主張。本框架不能保證 Agent 遵守原則，也不能取代實際權限控制、行為觀測及成果驗收。

不是萬國來朝，而是無 Bot 來朝。

## 核心內容

1. **P01–P23：工程行為治理。** 規範工程執行、驗收、故障恢復、責任、授權、安全及持續工作。
2. **G01–G16：組織協作治理。** 規範共同目標、分層自治、例外處理、方向校準及多 Agent 協作。
3. **PBT：可證偽驗證。** 分析規範及實際行為的一致性、適用條件、交互作用、反例與效益證據；測試設計與實際執行分別記錄。

完整、可獨立使用的規範見 [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)。39 條原則的 PBT 性質、交互作用、已執行範圍及限制見 [PBT.md](PBT.md)。有紀錄支持且已去識別化的工程故事見 [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md)。

## 使用方式

將 [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) 納入既有 Agent 指引，並為每項工作提供共同目標、必要限制、授權邊界及驗收條件。先確認執行者可取得適用規範，再以實際工具操作、狀態及交付成果評估行為；不以 Agent 自述作為唯一證據。使用 [PBT.md](PBT.md) 檢查規範與實際行為，並把 [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md) 當作工程經驗，不當成另一套規則。

## English

### What this project is

A principle-based governance framework for multi-agent work, independent of any particular model, platform, or technical architecture. It studies whether shared goals, layered autonomy, exception management, minimum sufficient context, and stage acceptance can reduce management overhead and engineering rework. These are research aims, not proven outcomes. The framework cannot guarantee agent compliance or replace access controls, observation of actual behavior, and acceptance of delivered results.

> I raise a hundred Bots so I need not serve a hundred Bots.

### The story

I have a small plot of my own, with a group of Bots on it.

I thought that having Bots would let me rest easy.

Instead, I spent every day reviewing reports, relaying orders, resolving disputes, and supervising engineering. Yesterday I directed them to build on Ubuntu; this morning, towers had appeared on Windows.

It took a night to build and half a day to tear down.

Then I understood:

Governing Bots is like governing a realm: I must neither handle every matter myself nor leave everything unattended.

So I established a constitution, clarified responsibilities, set direction, and granted autonomy.

For technical questions, solve them at the working level; for questions of direction, ask upward.

Know what is needed; there is no need to know everything.

Concentrate direction; distribute decisions.

I do not seek to decide everything myself. I seek a way for everything not to require my decision.

### Four rules of governance

- Work so people do not have to keep working.
- If the work is done, who did it is secondary.
- A philosophical slogan may be nothing more than an impressive piece of nonsense.
- Stop when enough is done; do no work without value.

The highest form of governance is not a court full of Bots, but a realm where no Bot needs to come to court.

The Emperor would like to sleep.

### Framework and use

- **P01–P23:** governance of engineering behavior, including acceptance, recovery, responsibility, authorization, safety, and continued work.
- **G01–G16:** governance of organizational collaboration, including shared goals, layered autonomy, exceptions, direction alignment, and multi-agent coordination.
- **PBT:** falsifiable analysis of the specification and observed behavior, including applicability, interactions, counterexamples, and outcome evidence. Test design is distinguished from work actually executed.

Read the standalone [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), the property-based testing analysis in [PBT.md](PBT.md), and the de-identified engineering history in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md). Adopt the governance text in existing agent instructions with explicit goals, constraints, authorization boundaries, and acceptance conditions. Evaluate actual tool use and delivered outcomes; do not rely only on an agent's claim that it complied.

This project builds on the engineering constitution in [AI-Engineering-Constitution](https://github.com/JonesLai25/AI-Engineering-Constitution), while adding organizational governance principles G01–G16 and PBT analysis. The earlier repository remains independent and unchanged.
